Shipping & Returns

How long will my order take?

Every gift is made just for you, so there are two parts:

  • Making it: 2-7 business days after we receive your pet's photo.
  • Shipping: 3-7 business days within the USA.

Most orders arrive within 1-2 weeks. Please allow up to 3 weeks during the busy holiday season. You'll get a tracking number by email as soon as your order ships.

🎄 Holiday orders: For delivery by December 25, please order and send your photo by December 4.

Where do you ship?

We currently ship within the United States only.

Shipping cost

A flat $6.99 per order anywhere in the US. Sales tax (if any) is shown at checkout before you pay.

Returns

Because every item is personalized with your pet, we can't accept returns or exchanges for a change of mind or the wrong size. Please double-check your order and photo before purchasing.

Damaged, defective, or misprinted?

We'll make it right. If your order arrives damaged, defective, or printed incorrectly:

  1. Email us within 30 days of delivery at adtherealone@yahoo.com.
  2. Include your order number and a clear photo of the problem.
  3. We'll send a free replacement or a full refund - your choice.

Lost package?

If tracking shows no movement for 10 business days, contact us and we'll help sort it out.
