Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Snoot & Co. respects your privacy. This page explains, in plain language, what information we collect and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Order details: your name, email address, and shipping address.
  • Your pet's photo: so we can create your custom gift.
  • Messages: anything you send us by email.

Payments

Payments are processed securely by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number. See Stripe's Privacy Policy.

How we use your information

  • To make, ship, and track your order.
  • To reply to your questions.

We never sell your information.

Who we share it with

Only the partners needed to complete your order:

  • Our print partner, which prints and ships your gift (receives your photo, name, and address).
  • Stripe, which processes your payment.
  • Shipping carriers, which deliver your package.

Your pet's photo

We use your photo only to make your order. We won't post it publicly or use it in ads without your permission. You can ask us to delete it at any time after your order ships.

Website data

Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically log basic technical information (such as your IP address and browser type) for security. We don't use advertising trackers.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct, or delete your information at any time by emailing adtherealone@yahoo.com.

Changes

If we update this policy, we'll change the date at the top of this page.
