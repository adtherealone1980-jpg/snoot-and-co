# 🐾 Snoot & Co.

Personalized gifts for pet lovers - custom mugs, ornaments, and keepsakes made
with your pet's photo. Printed on demand in the USA.

## How this website works
- `index.html` - the homepage
- `styles.css` - colors, fonts, and layout
- `products.js` - **the product list** (edit this to change products, prices, or Buy links)
- `shop.js` - builds the product cards from the list
- `shipping-returns.html`, `privacy.html`, `contact.html` - info pages

Payments are handled by Stripe Payment Links. No secret keys are stored here.
Products are made and shipped by a print-on-demand partner.
